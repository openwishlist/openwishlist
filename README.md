## Why OpenWishlist?

Keep wishlists close to home, without handing your plans to another platform. OpenWishlist is designed for a focused, low-friction experience that you can run on your own infrastructure.

## Features

- Create multiple wishlists and add items with notes, priorities, and links.
- Mark items as acquired and keep your lists tidy.
- Use a responsive interface that works comfortably on desktop and mobile.
- Self-host your data with the included Docker setup.
